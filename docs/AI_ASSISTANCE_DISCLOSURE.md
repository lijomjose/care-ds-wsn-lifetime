# AI-assistance disclosure

The authors conceived the research problem, mathematical model, CARE-DS
algorithm, comparator protocol, experimental design, interpretation, and
conclusions.

OpenAI Codex was used under the authors' direction to implement their
algorithmic specifications, prepare regression and validation tests, execute
and check statistical-analysis and table/figure-generation scripts, generate
computational reports, assist with language editing, and prepare the IEEE
Access LaTeX source. Its use therefore affected the software artifact, the
statistical outputs, tables and figures reported in the Results section, and
editorial wording throughout the manuscript.

The authors inspected the implementation, reran the validation workflow,
checked the numerical results and references, revised the generated text, and
accept full responsibility for the accuracy, originality, and integrity of the
work. No AI system is listed as an author or treated as a source of scientific
evidence.
